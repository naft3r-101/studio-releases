# Studio music library, v1

The tracks Studio offers under a video in its **Music** dialog. Studio downloads
a track from here the first time it is used and checks it against the SHA-256
in its own catalogue.

**Every track is CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication): free for any use,
including paid ads, with no fee and no credit required. All come from
[FreePD](https://freepd.com) (closed 2025), checked against its archived
category pages; the files are the originals, unaltered.
